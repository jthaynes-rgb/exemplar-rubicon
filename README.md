# Exemplar Rubicon — Architect Manifest

**Local-first desk architecture for builders who still own their metal.**

This repository is the public, scrubbed architectural surface of the Exemplar Rubicon method and the local sovereign desk design. It is not a dump of private product trees. Proprietary source, personal identifiers, secrets, and unpublished doctrine are not here and will not be added.

## What this is

A reproducible blueprint peers can inspect, fork, and adapt:

1. **Intent → pure tasks → typed confirm → seal** — the method.
2. **Drift discipline** — keep execution under a hard geometric bound.
3. **Local-first desk** — the operator is the only actor; seats and workers are instruments.
4. **Hash-chained seals** — finished work gets a fingerprint on one unbroken timeline.

## What this is not

- Not a social feed strategy.
- Not a claim that every security goal is already proven (see `SECURITY.md` — goals until sealed PASS).
- Not the private suite repositories.
- Not personal or proprietary material.
