# Tarot lattice — sentence drivers for Chapter 1

The cards are a way of choosing what a sentence *does* before the reader is told a system. Golden Dawn attributions are the working set, because they are the set that ties trump, Hebrew letter, element or planet or sign, and a path on the Tree into one gesture. Crowley’s later counterchange is recorded second, so a writer does not mix the two in one breath and call the mix “classical.”

Feel first. The name of the card does not belong in the line.

## 1. What is classical here, and what is a later fixing

**Letter classes are older than the tarot trumps.** Westcott’s *Sepher Yetzirah* states the skeleton this lattice inherits ([text](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Sepher_Yetzirah/Sepher_Yetzirah)):

- Twenty-two letters: three mothers, seven doubles, twelve simples.
- Mothers: Aleph, Mem, Shin — air, water, fire. Air is the tongue of the balance between water and fire. In the year: temperate, cold, heat. In the person: chest, belly, head.
- Doubles: Beth, Gimel, Daleth, Kaph, Pé, Resh, Tau. Each has a hard and a soft sound and a pair of opposites (life/death, peace/war, wisdom/folly, riches/poverty, grace/indignation, fertility/desolation, power/servitude). They are set over the seven planets, the seven days, and the seven gates of the soul. The Westcott text lists the planets as Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Saturn, Jupiter, and Mars. It does **not**, in the passage read here, lock each letter to one planet. Manuscript families disagree on that lock. Do not pretend *Sepher Yetzirah* itself prints the Golden Dawn planet row.
- Simples: Hé, Vau, Zain, Cheth, Teth, Yod, Lamed, Nun, Samekh, Ayin, Tzaddi, Qoph. Twelve properties (sight, hearing, smell, speech, taste, sexual love, work, movement, anger, mirth, imagination, sleep) and twelve directions. The zodiacal sequence used on the trumps is the later tarot fixing, not a sentence copied from this translation.

**The trump-to-letter lock is Golden Dawn.** *Book T*, “A Description of the Cards of the Tarot with their Attributions,” is the Order’s tarot paper, written in the main by S. L. MacGregor Mathers around the early 1890s, with Felkin material in the copy circulated as Mathers and Felkin ([PDF](https://benebellwen.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/mathers-and-felkin-golden-dawn-book-t-the-tarot-1888.pdf); dating and Regardie publication noted at [steve-p.org](https://steve-p.org/cards/GDaw.html) and [Hermetic Library](https://library.hrmtc.com/2020/12/25/three-books-of-golden-dawn-tarot/)). *Liber 777* (1909; tables later reprinted) is Crowley’s column-book of the same Golden Dawn correspondence set: about 191 columns and 35 rows, one row for each sephirah or path ([Hermetic Library contents](https://hermetic.com/crowley/libers/lib777), [collection note](https://hermetic.com/crowley/777/index), [Book 4 appendix](https://hermetic.com/crowley/book-4/app5), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/777_and_Other_Qabalistic_Writings_of_Aleister_Crowley)). A preface to an electronic *777 Revised* states that the correspondence tables facsimile the 1909 edition via the 1955 *777 Revised* ([PDF note](http://93beast.fea.st.user.fm/files/section1/777/Liber%20777%20Revised.pdf)).

This file does **not** reproduce those copyrighted columns. It states the working set densify needs, and it says where a row was spot-checked. Before a verse treats one row as immovable, open *777* Table I.

Earlier French occult tarot arranged the Fool and the Justice/Strength pair on a different plan. That earlier order was **not** reconstructed in this pass. Do not “correct” the table below from memory of another deck in the same paragraph.

## 2. Golden Dawn working set — majors as sentence drivers

Planet row used here is the Golden Dawn / *777* row, not a claim that every *Sepher Yetzirah* manuscript prints it:

| Double letter | Planet | Trump in this set |
|---------------|--------|-------------------|
| Beth | Mercury | Magician |
| Gimel | Moon | High Priestess |
| Daleth | Venus | Empress |
| Kaph | Jupiter | Wheel of Fortune |
| Pé | Mars | Tower |
| Resh | Sun | Sun |
| Tau | Saturn | Universe (World) |

Mothers: Aleph Air / Fool; Mem Water / Hanged Man; Shin Fire / Judgement. The other twelve trumps take the twelve simples in zodiacal order, Aries through Pisces, which is how Strength lands on Leo and Justice on Libra.

Paths below are the Golden Dawn Tree as used with that set (path 11 Aleph joins Kether–Chokmah, and so on). *777* also carries supplementary rows often marked 31 bis and 32 bis (the electronic preface’s table fragment shows 32 bis as Earth and 31 bis as breath/Akasa). Those are not extra trumps. Do not invent a twenty-third major for them.

The “sentence job” column is craft for this Codex. It is not a divinatory keyword from *Book T*.

*Book T*’s own divinatory voice, spot-checked, treats the Fool as idea and transcendence when the question is spiritual, and as folly when the question is material, and it treats early trumps as skill, change, beauty, war, teaching, inspiration, triumph, and a justice that arrests force ([PDF](https://benebellwen.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/mathers-and-felkin-golden-dawn-book-t-the-tarot-1888.pdf)). Use that as a check on tone, not as copy to paste. The Codex is not a divination manual.

### Path as joinery, not as scenery

A path is what the sentence joins. Priestess (Kether–Tiphareth) can carry a high quiet into the middle of the person without naming either station. Tower (Netzach–Hod) cuts a desire against an analysis. Universe (Yesod–Malkuth) fastens an image to a material close. If the sentence joins nothing — if it only decorates — it is not using the path.

## 3. Thelemic counterchange — state it, do not smear it

*Liber AL* I:57, in the Hermetic Library text of the New Comment: “All these old letters of my Book are aright; but צ is not the Star.” The comment then says Tzaddi is the letter of The Emperor, trump IV, and Hé is the Star, trump XVII, and that Aquarius and Aries are counterchanged, revolving on Pisces, just as Leo and Libra revolve about Virgo in trumps VIII and XI ([chapter I](https://hermetic.com/legis/new-comment/chapter-i)).

That is the primary statement read for this pack. Later readers do not agree what, physically, moved:

- One reading, repeated on [Tarot History Forum threads](https://www.tarotforum.net/threads/the-star-card.52816/page-14) and in a page that walks the symmetry argument ([nofaithinthehumanrace.com](https://nofaithinthehumanrace.com/?changemode=1&show777=1)): the Emperor takes Tzaddi, the Star takes Hé, and the signs counterchange. The same symmetry is why Adjustment (scales) and Lust (lion) can keep their pictures while their numbers sit at VIII and XI, so the numbers turn about Virgo the way Aries and Aquarius turn about Pisces.
- Another reading, argued on a Thelemic forum, keeps the signs on the old cards and moves the letters on the paths, or the reverse, and then notices that this fights the *Sepher Yetzirah* habit “Hé = Aries, Tzaddi = Aquarius” ([College & Temple of Thelema thread](https://thelema.org/forum/topic/13531/tzaddi-is-not-the-star)).

**Writer rule.** Pick one lattice for a passage.

- Golden Dawn sentence: Emperor feels like a ram, a first cut, a bound set at the start of a campaign. Star feels like a wide, cool distance after wreckage.
- Thelemic sentence: those two weathers may be counterchanged, and Strength/Justice may be numbered in the older VIII/XI picture-order (lion later, scales earlier) while the Hebrew letters stay with Leo and Libra. Thoth *names* used for that later deck — Magus, Adjustment, Fortune, Lust, Art, Aeon, Universe — belong only in a passage that has chosen the Thelemic lattice. Do not put “Lust” and “Strength VIII = Teth” in the same line.

The 1944 *Book of Thoth* was not copied into this pack. The symmetry argument above is taken from the New Comment plus the two secondary pages just cited. Do not invent further Thoth doctrine to settle the forum dispute. For Chapter 1 sovereignty prose, the Golden Dawn Emperor (a bound, Aries, a stated intent) is the more direct driver. Use the counterchange only when a passage is deliberately about a letter that will not stay in its old house — a reveal, not a default.

## 4. Minors — weather inside a paragraph, not a second plot

### Four worlds and the ten

A hand-copied Golden Dawn minor paper published by Mary K. Greer (Sub Spe / J. W. Brodie-Innes) places the numbered cards on the Tree in the four worlds ([Greer, 2008](https://marykgreer.com/2008/03/04/the-golden-dawn-minor-arcana/)):

- Wands: the Tree in Atziluth.
- Cups: the Tree in Briah.
- Pentacles: the Tree in Assiah.

The excerpt captured here did not include the Swords header. In the same system the remaining world is Yetzirah, and Swords are the air suit that belongs there. Confirm that header in the article or in *Book T* before a verse depends on it. Do not invent a fifth world.

Number is sephirah: ace as root (Kether), two Chokmah, three Binah, four Chesed, five Geburah, six Tiphareth, seven Netzach, eight Hod, nine Yesod, ten Malkuth. Greer’s notes use that numbering explicitly (two as Chokmah, three as Binah, four as Chesed, ten as Malkuth).

**Sentence use.** An ace is almost pure element, nearly no plot. A two joins. A three establishes. A six is a heart-beat in the middle of the matter. A ten completes and can clog. One minor force per paragraph is enough. A chapter that names ten pips has become a catalogue.

### Decans — the generative rule, spot-checked

Golden Dawn pips from two through ten take the thirty-six decans in Chaldean order, beginning with Mars in the first decan of Aries on the Two of Wands. That start is independently stated: “Lord of Dominion,” Mars in Aries ([Greer](https://marykgreer.com/2008/03/04/the-golden-dawn-minor-arcana/); [Still Small Voice](https://stillsmallvoicetarot.com/tarot-decan-walk-2-of-wands-aries-1/); [Joy Vernon](https://joyvernon.com/two-of-wands-mars-in-aries/)).

Those titles match the grid. Cells not named in an excerpt above are the rule applied. If a verse depends on an unchecked title (“Lord of …” for a pip this pack did not quote), read *Book T* for that card before using the title. The weather — Mars in Aries versus Moon in Cancer — is the part that changes a sentence. The title is optional and usually should stay off the page.

**How a decan changes cadence.** Mars in Aries (2 of Wands) is a sentence that claims a field. Moon in Cancer (4 of Cups) is a sentence that receives, then sours toward an end. Saturn in Leo would be a fire sentence under a weight. Jupiter in Capricorn (2 of Pentacles) is material change that stays rhythmic rather than fruitful — Greer’s note says harmonious change but no product. One decan colors one paragraph. Do not stack three decans to look learned.

### Courts — voices, not characters you introduce by rank

Greer’s minor notes treat the Two as uniting King and Queen, the Three as producing the Prince, the Four as producing the Princess. That is the Golden Dawn family theory: the courts are the persons of the suit, not a separate plot.

For sentence work, four voices are enough:

- The fiery court of a suit charges.
- The watery court receives and holds.
- The airy court sorts and aims.
- The earthy court is the throne of the material — the desk, the body, the tool in the hand.

Thoth’s later rename (Knight / Queen / Prince / Princess in place of the older King / Knight / Page layout) is part of the Thelemic deck, not of the Golden Dawn paper Greer prints. Do not mix the titles in one paragraph. Chapter 1 does not need a court onstage. If a voice is required, let the syntax carry it: a charge has verbs of onset; a reception has verbs of keeping; a sorting has verbs of distinction; a material throne has nouns you could pick up.

## 5. What Chapter 1 should not do with this lattice

- Do not print card names, path numbers, or Hebrew letters in the prose to prove the lattice is there.
- Do not open on the Hierophant. A first sentence that transmits a creed breaks feel-before-know and breaks the book’s public refusal to be a religion.
- Do not use the Tower as a tantrum paragraph. One stroke, then the Star or the Sun, or silence.
- Do not moralize the Devil. Name the chain (a platform, a habit, a tool, a desk) and keep moving.
- Do not resolve the Hanged Man or the Moon in the same sentence that poses them. The knowing sentence comes later, and it is usually the Sun, the Hermit, or Adjustment.
- Do not import Thoth names (Aeon, Lust, Art, Magus) into a passage that is still on the Golden Dawn Emperor = Aries setting.
- Do not define unpublished densify cadence-terms in order to “attach” them to a trump. Those terms are out of scope for this pack. The brief’s examples are Nexus and He-Confluence. Leave them unnamed.
