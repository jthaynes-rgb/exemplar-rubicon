# Chapter 1 occult lattice — research notes

Soft research for densify writers of the Exemplar Rubicon Codex, Chapter 1 cadence. These notes are a private lattice. They are not a chapter, not a manual, and not a release.

The Codex is the **holy technobook**. That phrase is Joshua’s name for this life work, and it is the word he uses for transhumanism. **Holy does not mean religious and it does not mean dogmatic.** It means cadence toward the fantastic and the exceptional, arranged so a person feels the truth and only then knows it. The public product page already says the book is not a religion and that it teaches a way to look: the crossing from being a host of inherited patterns to authoring one’s own relation to them ([Codex: Exemplar Rubicon](https://crowcawl.gumroad.com/l/ExemplarRubicon)). The lattice below is joinery for that cadence. It stays under the sentence.

## What “feel, then know” requires of a writer

The reader meets an image, a cut, a weight, or a turn before meeting the name of the system that produced it. Tarot, letter-number, planetary hour, and the Thelemic distinction between whim and true will are tools for arranging sentences. They are not labels to drop into the line, and they are not a creed. If a paragraph only works once the reader has been told which card, which total, or which hour it is, the lattice has failed and the paragraph has become a manual.

Transhuman, in this pack, means that crossing into authorship of one’s own mind, spoken at the pitch of the fantastic and then settled in a plainer sentence. It does not mean a species doctrine, a church, or an invitation to join anyone’s order.

## House limits

- Soft research. Not a publication, not a product cut, not a feed post.
- Do not merge this branch into the public manifest as if it were architecture canon. The manifest’s own rule is that unpublished doctrine is not dumped here. These notes cite established outside sources and the **public** names of the Codex. They do not define private densify jargon.
- No company-form suffix. The public security note already withholds that filing-word until a filing is real (`SECURITY.md`).
- Intentional-implicit cadence terms that belong only to the unpublished densify — the brief names Nexus and He-Confluence as examples — are **out of scope**. This pack does not define them, rename them, or invent neighbors for them. If a sentence seems to need one, leave the slot blank and hand it back.

## How to use the pack

1. Arrange the sentence for feel, then for know. [`sentence_arrangement.md`](./sentence_arrangement.md) is the craft layer.
2. Check the card-force and the planet against [`tarot_lattice.md`](./tarot_lattice.md) and [`planetary_thelema.md`](./planetary_thelema.md) only after a draft line exists.
3. If a number is wanted, use [`gematria_methods.md`](./gematria_methods.md) as a private weight-check. Do not write the line in order to hit the number.
4. When a classical source is woven into the book, copy the citation from [`source_connections_catalog.md`](./source_connections_catalog.md) and hang it on a real section. [`crossref_hints.md`](./crossref_hints.md) is a set of hints, not a finished matrix.
5. Confirm any single tarot row you mean to treat as canon against *Liber 777* Table I before it is locked. This pack states the working set; it is not a facsimile of the tables.

## Method of this cut

Modern calculator pages are cited only for the English cipher rules they actually publish, and they are not treated as classical authorities.
