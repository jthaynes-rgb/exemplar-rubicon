# Crossref hints — classical sources to public EXR loci

Fanatic fills `CROSSREF_MATRIX`. This file does not. Every densify section number below is **unstated**. Unpublished densify jargon is **out of scope**. The brief’s examples are Nexus and He-Confluence. They are not given rows, aliases, or definitions.

A hint is a likely hang-point on a **public** name. It is not a claim that the verse, diagram, or book-section already says the classical thing. Against the Monolith and Adapt or Die are the named ends of Part Three on the product page (S37). Their verses were not read. Books I–IX and Appendices I–III have no public titles in this pass. Do not invent those titles in the matrix. Leave a blank and a source id.

In-repo loci (not S-numbers): `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/DRIFT.md`, `docs/SEAL.md`, `SECURITY.md`.

## How to fill one matrix cell

1. Copy the S-number and the bibliographic line from `source_connections_catalog.md`.
2. Hang it on a real section you can open. If the only warrant in this file is a title, mark the cell `title-hint` and do not quote imaginary verses.
3. Prefer one primary source per cell. S07, S08, S10, S12, S13, S20, S21, S22, S26, S27, S29 are supports. They should sit in a note under a primary, not as a second pillar.
4. Leave the densify § blank until you have the section in front of you.
